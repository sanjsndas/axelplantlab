🍪 digital crumbs & seedling sensors

🌾 Last updated: May 1, 2025 | Cookie Version 2.0

Just like a garden journal keeps track of seasonal changes, our website uses small data files — cookies — to remember your preferences and make your experience bloom. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which ones we plant, and how you tend to your digital plot.

🍪 1. What Are Cookies? (the composting explanation)

Cookies are tiny text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit a website. They help the site recognize your device, remember your settings (like light/dark mode or language), and understand how visitors interact with our content. At Axel Plant Lab, we treat cookies like beneficial insects: they improve the ecosystem without ever harming your privacy. We never use invasive tracking or sell cookie-generated data.

🌿 2. Why We Use Cookies — a regenerative approach

Our cookies have clear purposes rooted in respect for your digital sovereignty:

  • Essential (strictly necessary): These cookies keep the website functioning — navigation, secure forms, session management. Without them, our plant clinic scheduler and contact forms wouldn’t work properly.
  • Functional (preference cookies): Remember your choices, such as preferred text size, video embed settings, or dismissed pop-ups. They make returning to Axel Plant Lab feel like stepping into a familiar greenhouse.
  • Analytics (privacy-first): We use lightweight, anonymized tracking (like Plausible or self-hosted analytics) to know which plant guides are most popular and whether any pages are slow. No personal identifiers, no cross-site tracking, and we never share raw data with advertisers.

🌻 A note on third-party cookies: Occasionally we embed videos (e.g., from YouTube on our propagation tutorials) or map location (OpenStreetMap). These services may set their own cookies if you choose to interact with them. We don't control those, but we avoid unnecessary embeds and encourage you to review their policies. Most embeds are set to “privacy-enhanced mode” whenever possible.

⚙️ 4. Your Cookie Preferences (harvesting control)

You are always in charge of your digital plot. You can manage or delete cookies in several ways:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings (e.g., Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality — like not being able to complete the contact form or join a waiting list.
  • Our preference tool: You can adjust your non-essential cookie choices at any time using the button below. This will store your new preference and refresh the page.

🍃 Manage your cookie preferences — choose to enable or disable non-essential analytics & functional cookies (except necessary ones).

Your current preference is stored locally. Changes take effect immediately.

📜 5. Consent & Transparency (sowing trust)

When you first visit our website, you will see a small banner (the "cookie notice") asking for your acceptance of non-essential cookies. By clicking "Got it, grow on" or continuing to browse, you agree to our use of essential and functional cookies, plus optional analytics as described. However, you may withdraw your consent at any time using the button above or by clearing your browser cookies. We never assume consent without action — our default is minimal data collection.

For users in jurisdictions requiring explicit opt-in (like the GDPR), we only set analytics cookies after you give active consent. Essential cookies are always active because they keep the website secure and operational.

🌍 6. Third-Party Embeds and External Links

We occasionally link to external resources — for instance, recommended organic seed suppliers, research articles, or our Instagram gallery. Those websites have independent privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to read. Embedded content from platforms like Vimeo (for workshop recordings) may set cookies if you choose to play the video. However, we use "no-cookie" URLs where possible. By interacting with embed content, you are subject to that third party's terms.

🛡️ 7. Managing Cookies Across Devices

If you use multiple devices (phone, laptop, tablet), each browser manages cookies separately. To fully control your cookie footprint, adjust preferences on each device and browser individually. Our preference tool stores your decision locally, and you can always revisit this Cookies Policy page for updated guidance.
