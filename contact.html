🌿 the heart of our lab: real conversations, real dirt

At Axel Plant Lab, every message is read by a horticulturist, not a bot. Whether you’re battling persistent aphids on your kale, curious about no-dig gardening, or longing to identify that mysterious mushroom in your woodchip path — our team offers science-backed, soulful advice. We’ve built more than a website; we nurture a growing community of soil stewards, balcony farmers, and heirloom guardians. Over 78,000 words of unique content on our platform are only the beginning. When you reach out, you become part of a living ecosystem of knowledge exchange.

Our physical nursery sprawls across 2.5 acres in Grove Valley: you’ll find over 200 varieties of native perennials, a dedicated composting hub, and a tiny seed library where you can borrow & return seeds. We host monthly “Weeding & Wisdom” workshops where beginners learn to distinguish lamb’s quarters from pigweed. And yes, our lab includes a small microscopy station to analyze soil biota. Contact us to schedule a one-on-one consultation, or simply come by to say hello and pet our garden cat, “Compost Carl”.

📋 quick reference: what we help with

  • 🌻 custom planting plans (permaculture guilds & polycultures)
  • 🍅 diagnosing leaf spots, nutrient deficiencies, and phytotoxicity
  • 🌧️ rain garden design & erosion control strategies
  • 🧪 soil testing interpretation (pH, organic matter, heavy metals)
  • 🐞 biological pest control & companion planting layouts
  • 🌿 propagation workshops (grafting, cuttings, division)
  • 🏡 balcony food forest design (vertical, dwarf varieties)

All consultations reflect our regenerative ethos: we never recommend synthetic chemicals. Instead, we build resilience.

💬 frequently asked questions — direct from inbox

Q: Do you ship seeds and plants internationally?
A: Currently we ship seeds (heirloom, organic) to US and Canada. Live plants are available for local pickup or regional delivery (within 80 miles). We’re working on expanding — sign up to our newsletter for updates.

Q: Can I bring a soil sample to the lab for analysis?
A: Absolutely! Drop-off hours: Wed 2–5pm or Sat 10am–1pm. Standard soil health panel ($25) includes organic matter, microbe activity score, and recommendations.

Q: How can I volunteer or intern at Axel Plant Lab?
A: We welcome volunteers for our demonstration gardens and seed-saving initiatives. Send an email with “Volunteer” in the subject line. Seasonal internships also available for agroecology students.

Q: My question is urgent — my tomato leaves are curling!
A: Don’t panic. Email us photos and we’ll reply within 24h. In the meantime, check for overwatering, herbicide drift, or heat stress. We’ll guide you through organic remedies.
