🔏 privacy rooted in respect

🌱 Last updated: May 1, 2025 | Version 2.3

At Axel Plant Lab — The Institute of Soil & Soul, your privacy is as vital as healthy soil. We believe in transparency, minimal data collection, and never selling your personal information. This policy explains how we care for your data like a tender seedling.

📜 1. Information We Gather (the compost of insight)

We collect only what helps us grow our community and respond to your gardening needs. This includes:

  • 🌱 Direct interactions: When you fill out our contact form, sign up for “The Root Letter” newsletter, or join a workshop, we may ask for your name, email address, phone number, and gardening interests. This enables us to answer your questions, send seasonal tips, and share event updates.
  • 📊 Automated data (respectful analytics): Like most gardens, our website uses cookies and similar technologies to understand which plant guides are popular and improve navigation. We never track you across third-party sites. All analytics are anonymized and used exclusively for enhancing your experience.
  • 🗺️ Location data (only if volunteered): When you provide your nursery pickup preference or request local climate advice, we may store general location (e.g., city/state) but never precise GPS without consent.

🌻 2. How We Use Your Green Data

Your information fuels our mission: regenerative gardening for everyone. Concretely, we use data for:

  • Responding to questions from our botanists (yes, real humans!) within 24-48 hours.
  • Sending “The Root Letter” — a monthly digest of propagation secrets, upcoming seed swaps, and soil health insights. You can unsubscribe anytime with one click.
  • Improving our educational content and lab offerings based on what our community cares about (most-loved topics: tomato troubleshooting, native pollinators, no-till methods).
  • Processing workshop registrations or plant clinic appointments securely.
  • Internal record-keeping and limited analytics to prevent spam or abuse.

🤝 Our promise: We will NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Not for money, not for exposure. Your trust is the most valuable nutrient in our ecosystem.

🍂 3. Cookies & Digital Soil Sensors

Our website uses essential and functional cookies to remember your preferences (e.g., dark/light mode preferences, session data). We also use a minimal set of analytics cookies via privacy-friendly services that don’t identify you personally. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings, but note that disabling certain cookies may affect navigation. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.

🌿 4. Sharing Information — Only When Needed

We may share your data only in limited scenarios:

  • Service providers: Trusted partners who help us send emails (newsletter platforms) or process payments (Stripe/PayPal). They are bound by strict data protection agreements and cannot use your data for any other purpose.
  • Legal compliance: If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of Axel Plant Lab, our visitors, or the public.
  • Business transitions: In the unlikely event of a merger or acquisition, you will be notified via email and website notice before any data transfer.

Outside these cases, your information remains safely within our nursery.

🪴 5. Data Security — Like an Unbreakable Terrarium

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (SSL/TLS), access restrictions, and routine monitoring. Your contact form submissions are transmitted over secure channels, and our staff undergoes annual privacy training. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure — we encourage you to protect your passwords and let us know immediately if you suspect any breach of your information.

💧 6. Your Rights As a Plant-People Ally

Depending on your location (including GDPR for EU residents and CCPA for California), you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your data (“right to be forgotten”).
  • Restrict or object to certain processing (e.g., direct marketing).
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please email privacy@axelplantlab.com or write to the address in the footer. We’ll respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing requests — just like checking a plant’s ID before repotting.

🌾 7. Children’s Privacy (Little Sprouts Matter)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. Our content is family-friendly, but if we become aware that we’ve inadvertently gathered data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately. Parents or guardians may contact us to request removal.

📮 8. Third-Party Links & Embedded Content

Our website may include links to external sites (e.g., recommended organic suppliers, YouTube videos). Those websites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. Please review their policies before sharing information. Embedded content (like our OpenStreetMap location) functions as if you visited the third-party website and may collect data about your interaction.

✉️ 9. Email Communications & Newsletters

When you subscribe to “The Root Letter” or request information, we’ll send you gardening wisdom, event invites, and nursery updates. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. Even after unsubscribing, we may retain limited transactional data (e.g., workshop registration history) as needed for legal or operational reasons.

🔄 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates when the latest revision was made. If changes are significant, we’ll notify you via email or a prominent notice on our website. Your continued use of the site after modifications means you accept the updated policy.

At Axel Plant Lab, personal data is treated with the same reverence we give to heirloom seeds — protected, cherished, and never exploited. Thank you for trusting us with your story.
